Who I Am

Driven by communication,
grounded in action.

I'm Riaz Khan, a first-year BSc Speech and Language Therapy student at the University of Roehampton, and for me SLT and law have always felt like the same interest — two fields built entirely on what language can and cannot do. In a clinic that might mean giving a child the tools to be understood. In a legal context it might mean ensuring someone's voice actually counts.

Outside my degree I've been getting as much real experience as I can — insight programmes with Goldman Sachs, PwC, Linklaters, TLT, BDO, and Barclays, a hands-on SLT placement at a primary school, running my own football sessions in East London, and building a TikTok to over 50,000 followers through consistent content.

I'm also an award-winning public speaker. Winning the Jack Petchey Speak Out Award is probably the best single summary of what I'm actually about: taking something that matters and making it land.
