Who I Am

Driven by communication,
grounded in action.

I'm Riaz Khan, a first-year BSc Speech and Language Therapy student at the University of Roehampton, and for me SLT and law have always felt like the same interest. Both come down to what language can do — how it shapes outcomes, protects rights, and changes what people are able to do in the world. In a clinical room that might mean giving a child the tools to be understood. In a legal context it might mean ensuring someone's voice actually counts. That through-line is what I keep coming back to.

Outside my degree I've been getting as much real experience as I can. I've completed insight programmes with Goldman Sachs, PwC, Linklaters, TLT, BDO, and Barclays, done a hands-on SLT observation placement at a primary school, led my own community football sessions, and built experience across healthcare, retail, and community settings. None of it feels unrelated to me.

I'm also an award-winning public speaker, which is probably the best single summary of what I'm actually about: communicating something that matters, and making it land.
