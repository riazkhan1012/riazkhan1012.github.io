Who I Am

Driven by communication,
grounded in action.

I'm Riaz Khan, a first-year Speech and Language Therapy student at the University of Roehampton. I've always been drawn to communication — not just as a clinical skill, but as the thing that shapes how people are heard, understood, and treated. That's what pulled me toward SLT, and it's what keeps pulling me toward law and advocacy too.

Outside of my degree, I've spent a lot of time just getting out there. I've volunteered in healthcare and retail, organised charity events, and taken part in insight programmes with firms like Goldman Sachs, PwC, Linklaters, and Barclays. I don't see any of it as unrelated — it's all building the same thing.

I'm also an award-winning public speaker. Winning the Jack Petchey Speak Out Award is something I'm genuinely proud of, and it's probably the best single example of what I'm about.
