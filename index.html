Who I Am

Driven by communication,
grounded in action.

I'm Riaz Khan, a first-year BSc Speech and Language Therapy student at the University of Roehampton, and for me SLT and law have always felt like the same interest — two fields built entirely on what language can and cannot do. In a clinic that might mean giving a child the tools to be understood. In a legal context it might mean ensuring someone's voice actually counts. The connection between the two is what keeps pulling me forward in both directions.

Outside my degree I've been getting as much real experience as I can — insight programmes with Goldman Sachs, PwC, Linklaters, TLT, BDO, and Barclays, a hands-on SLT placement at a primary school, running my own football sessions in East London (I'm based in West London), and building a TikTok to over 50,000 followers through consistent content. None of it feels unrelated to me.

I'm also an award-winning public speaker. Winning the Jack Petchey Speak Out Award is probably the best single summary of what I'm actually about: taking something that matters and making it land.
